# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This project exists to develop, maintain, document, and support the software in
this repository. Project spaces are for **productive discussion directly related
to that work**.

## Stay on Topic

Discussion must concern:

- **This project**, its documentation, or its development.
- Bugs, features, security, testing, packaging, or support.
- Another piece of software when its integration, compatibility, dependency,
  migration, or technical behavior is **directly relevant** to this project.

**Political advocacy**, **religious debate**, **culture-war discussion**, and
promotion of unrelated personal beliefs or causes belong elsewhere.

A political, legal, or social subject may be mentioned only when it has a
**specific and necessary technical effect** on the software. Keep such
discussion limited to that effect.

## Expected Conduct

- Discuss **the work**, not the person doing it.
- Give useful, **evidence-based** technical feedback.
- Remain civil when disagreeing.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about project scope.
- **Stop** when a maintainer tells you that a discussion is off-topic.

## Unacceptable Conduct

The following are **not permitted**:

- Racism, racial slurs, or attacks based on personal or demographic traits.
- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or publication of private information.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, ragebait, flamebait, or deliberate
  provocation.
- Repeatedly derailing, reopening, or spreading the same dispute.
- Political, religious, or ideological campaigning.
- Spam, unrelated promotion, or recruitment.
- Attacking unrelated projects, communities, or their contributors.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to **this repository** and its official issue
tracker, pull requests, discussions, chat rooms, and other project-managed
spaces.

It does **not** regulate participants' private lives, affiliations, or beliefs
outside official project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may hide or remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions,
issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

**Severe violations** may result in immediate removal. Maintainers have **final
authority** over project scope and moderation.

Report violations privately to **krisyotam@protonmail.com**.
